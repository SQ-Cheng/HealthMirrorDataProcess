# Study result audit (2026-09-29)

## Paper study mapping

| Paper section | Source directories and role |
|---|---|
| Study 1: Inpatient laboratory trajectories | `study/exp2_lab_longitudinal_statistics` supplies descriptive longitudinal analyses, not facial-prediction outcomes. |
| Study 2: Single-time-point physiological prediction | The three face/history regression directories, their corresponding binary-classification directories, and `study/exp3_video_lab_regression`. Only source-aligned holdouts or explicitly matched five-fold protocols are contrasted. |
| Study 3: Paired-face laboratory change | `study/exp6_face_pair_lab_delta` and `study/exp6_face_pair_lab_delta_classifacation` provide separately trained regression and direction models; `study/exp7_representation_similarity` provides exploratory, training-free feature analysis. |
| Study 4: Postoperative status from facial recordings | `study/exp4`, `study/exp5_face_pair_recovery`, and `study/exp5_face_pair_casus` address distinct constructed or laboratory-linked postoperative outcomes. The two partial-CASUS protocols use different laboratory snapshots. |
| Study 5: ECG signal-quality control | `study/exp1_sqa_pretrain` supplies an independent weak-label ECG evaluation; it is not a clinical comparison with the facial-video cohort. |

Repository names retain their historical `exp` prefixes only for traceability; the paper uses the study taxonomy above.
The manuscript is organized directly by these five studies, with each study's protocol and findings together rather than separate Methods and Results sections.

## Headline numbers and interpretation boundaries

- Exp2 original holdout: face-only hemoglobin regression n=366, R2=0.164, r=0.426, MAE=14.608 g/L; face+history R2=0.770, r=0.880; history-only R2=0.774, r=0.886. For creatinine, R2=0.021/0.529/0.150 in that same order. The history comparison is on the aligned per-target holdout, not a causal estimate of facial contribution.
- Exp2 5-fold pooled OOF: face-only hemoglobin regression n=1,897, R2=0.244, r=0.503; patient-diverse 30/40 R2=0.260, r=0.518; Exp3 video R2=0.197, r=0.469. Lactate R2=0.116/0.148/0.063 in the same order. These are different trained models tested on the same fold-held-out video records, with no claim of statistical significance from point differences alone.
- Exp2 original holdout binary: face-only hemoglobin n=366, AUROC=0.720, bACC=0.698; face+history AUROC=0.905, bACC=0.802; history-only AUROC=0.924, bACC=0.845. Face-only 5-fold pooled OOF hemoglobin AUROC=0.778, bACC=0.714. Other face-only classification targets often have bACC near 0.5.
- Exp3 original holdout hemoglobin n=366, R2=0.166, r=0.442, MAE=14.826 g/L; pooled OOF n=1,897, R2=0.197. Duration/sampler ablations did not produce an across-target uniform improvement.
- Exp6 original holdout delta regression: hemoglobin n=182, MAE=10.511 g/L, R2=0.325, r=0.571; lactate n=104, MAE=0.732 mmol/L, R2=0.193, r=0.450; O2Hb n=105, R2=0.149. Urea, troponin, platelets, creatinine and total bilirubin have negative or approximately zero R2. Direction classification is a separate trained model, not thresholded regression.
- Source-count definitions differ: Exp2/Exp3 `n` is held-out **videos**; Exp6 `n` is held-out **ordered pairs**; Exp4/Exp5 `n` is held-out postoperative videos; longitudinal n is patients/episodes; Exp1 n is ECG windows. Never total these as independent patients.
- The 5-fold split search is a new protocol rather than a replay of the original split. Fold-specific robust scalers and binary class weights are fitted on the training partition. The four selected CV protocols use matching task records and fold assignments. CV training seeds are fold-specific; original runs used different seed derivation.

## Checks before publication

1. Every table or figure must state whether it is original held-out test or pooled 5-fold out-of-fold evaluation. Do not mix these within one numerical contrast without labeling.
2. Give units for MAE. AUROC and bACC are unitless; the binary positive definition is target-specific. Exp6 direction `positive` means later value exceeds earlier value, independent of whether that is clinically favorable.
3. No external validation, uncertainty interval, or clinical causality is supported by current result files. Prefer descriptive point estimates and explicitly identify small test sets.
4. Do not report the older paired partial-CASUS and current post-only partial-CASUS as a controlled ablation.
5. Do not describe weak-label Exp1 validation as human-annotated performance, or Exp4's clock-based target as independently measured recovery.
